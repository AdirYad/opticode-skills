# Blueprint: building a sales page from the reader's questions

The system stays fixed: palette, type, motion, sheets, button. The content and the proof change. A cold visitor asks a chain of questions in order. Build one section per question; the order below is the default, the copy can change it.

## What makes a page work
1. **Show the thing, do not describe it.** Every claim has a real or moving artefact next to it.
2. **A real person stands behind it**, in several roles and photos.
3. **Feeling before facts, facts before money.**
4. **A button every 2-3 screens**, same look, same text.
5. **One signature effect per emotional beat**, not everywhere.
6. **Money is a story, told twice:** in the middle, and again in the closing sheet.

## The sequence

| # | The reader asks | Section | Kit block | For a non-visual product (service, course, SaaS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is this for me, and what do I get? | Hero | 1 or 18 | The phone or laptop shows the RESULT, chips show 3 outcomes as tiny live UI |
| 2 | Does this person get my problem? | Pain | 2 or 19 | Same, their pain in their words |
| 3 | Can I see it work? | Proof moment | 2 (video on `.plate`) or 18 | A 30-60s real screen recording |
| 4 | Will it fit my case? | Variants | 20 | Their outputs as 3-4 loops or screenshots |
| 5 | How much does it really save? | Scale | 21 or 4 | The command that triggers their system, then N outputs |
| 6 | I already tried things. Why is this different? | Tried, then the other way | 22 or 6 | Before/after of the same object |
| 7 | Who is behind this? | Authority | 3 | The owner's real path, real numbers |
| 8 | What will I have at the end? | Outcomes | 7 and 23 | Results, never lesson titles |
| 9 | How does it go? | Route | 8 | Art is the step's real screen where possible |
| 10 | Why does it work? | Mechanism | 14 | Reasons, with one real proof beside them |
| 11 | Is it right for someone like me? | For, not for | 10 | Same |
| 12 | Who else did this? | Proof wall | 11 | Real messages |
| 13 | What does it cost, and is it worth it? | Anchor, then offer | 12 and 13, with 9 | Same, prices from the brief |
| 14 | What if I regret it? | Guarantee | 15 | Same |
| 15 | I still have questions. | FAQ | 16 | Real proof inside the "will it work for me" answer |
| 16 | So what do I do now? | Choice, then close | 24 and 17 | Same |

## Making a non-visual product physical
- Show the **output**, not the process: the finished dashboard, the message that arrived, the site live, the money in the account.
- Record the **real screen** doing the thing, cut to 4-8s silent loops; one real demo with sound for the proof moment.
- Use **before/after** of the same object.
- Turn lists into **live UI**: typed prompts, cards that fill, ticks that draw, counters.
- Every number gets its **real screenshot**, taken fresh.
- If an asset does not exist: a marked `.slot` naming exactly what to record, and it goes on the missing-items list. Never a stand-in.

## The bar for a full page
`scripts/lp-gaps.mjs` blocks below 30 images, 6 proof items and 5 buttons. Aim well above that on a long page.

## Adapting the order
Keep 1, 2 and 13-16 almost always. 3-6 are the "show it" block: rebuild them around the product's output. For a short or cheap offer, drop 4, 5 or 9, never 6, 7 or 13.
