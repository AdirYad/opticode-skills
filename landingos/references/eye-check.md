# Eye check: what "this level" looks like

The gate proves nothing is broken. This file is how to judge beauty when looking at the section screenshots. Read every sheet, mobile first. Any "no" is a fix, then one more look.

**Density**
- Does every mobile screen hold something to look at besides text? Three screens of plain text in a row is a fail.
- Are there at least 3 scale-shock moments, type so large it stops the thumb?
- Does a sparse section follow every 2 dense ones?

**Colour and depth**
- Are there 2-3 full colour sheets on the page, spaced apart? A page that is dark all the way down is unfinished.
- Is the night tone a real hue? Pure black, or any section that reads flat grey, is a fail.
- Do neighbouring sections differ in sheet or texture?
- Do figures stand on a sun, never inside a rectangle?
- Do shadows fall downward and stay black? A coloured glow is a fail.

**Finish**
- Is there one button look on the whole page, solid with a real edge?
- Is any button sitting directly on a flare sheet? It must be inside a `.sheet`.
- Do both colours keep their jobs? Flare for action and emotion, field for structure.
- Is every heading one piece, with no orphan word on its last line?
- Are prices the largest thing in their sheet, sign before the digits, no line break inside?
- Is every image in one of the 5 frames from `imagery.md`? A bare rectangle reads as unfinished.
- Are there light assets that look pasted on? Re-render them dark.

**Hebrew**
- Does every section read right to left, with the eye starting top right?
- Are Latin words inside Hebrew lines in the right place, with punctuation on the correct side?

**Compare the first screen to the ad.** Cover everything below the fold and ask: in five seconds, what is this, who is it for, and what do I do next? If it takes longer, the page is not done.
