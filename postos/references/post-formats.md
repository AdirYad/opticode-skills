# Post shapes

If a channel's posts all share one skeleton, a reader knows the ending by the second line and stops reading. So a batch of N posts uses N different shapes. A numbered list gets used once per batch at most, and only if the items really are equals.

Shapes are grouped by what the reader leaves with. Use the English name in `format` fields. Bend a shape to the topic whenever the topic asks for it.

## The reader leaves with something to do

### Full Walkthrough
One use of one thing, explained so completely that the reader can do it today.
```
[The annoyance, or the outcome, that makes this worth reading]
[Where to start, two lines at most]
[The real action: the exact wording, the exact setting]
[What comes back, described precisely]
[The detail most people get wrong]
[What this replaces, in time or money]
[Ask]
```

### Hidden Switch
Something already within reach that hardly anyone knows about.
```
[Something the reader does the long way]
[It has been possible to skip that for a while]
[Where it is]
[The single use that makes it worth trying]
[Why it matters to this audience now]
[Ask]
```

## The reader leaves with a different opinion

### Unpopular Opinion
Popular advice, shown to be wrong or incomplete.
```
[The advice as people repeat it]
[One short line disagreeing]
[What actually happens, and the reason]
[One concrete case]
[What to do differently, from today]
[Ask]
```

### Retirement Notice
A habit that made sense once and doesn't any more. Sharp opening, generous body.
```
[The habit and what it costs]
[Why it used to be reasonable]
[What moved]
[The replacement, exactly]
[Ask]
```

## The reader leaves with evidence

### Field Report
First person, from something the buyer really tried, with the parts that went badly. Without the failures the praise isn't believable.
```
[What was tried and when]
[What was expected]
[What really happened, starting with the surprise]
[The point where it fell apart]
[Worth it for whom, not worth it for whom]
[Ask]
```

### One Client, Two Numbers
One real situation told from start to finish, measured at both ends. A client or result that doesn't exist disqualifies the post.
```
[Who, and the situation they were in]
[Where they started, with a figure]
[The one thing that changed]
[Where they ended up, with a figure]
[What the reader can take from that]
[Ask]
```

## The reader leaves with a decision

### Head to Head
Two options people mix up. What's worth reading is the judgement, not a feature table.
```
[The two options and the confusion between them]
[One honest line about each]
[When the first is right, when the second is right]
[Which one the buyer would take, and why]
[Ask]
```

### The Real Question
A question the audience keeps asking, answered once and properly. Only with a question that really came in.
```
[The question, as people put it]
[Why the usual answer doesn't help]
[The direct answer, in a line]
[The reasoning, or a case]
[Ask]
```

## The reader leaves understanding the news

### Behind the Buzz
Everyone saw it. Nobody explained what to do about it.
```
[The thing that's everywhere right now]
[What it really is, in a line]
[What almost everyone is missing]
[What to do with that, concretely]
[Ask]
```

## Picking shapes for a batch
| what the topic has | shapes that fit |
|---|---|
| one strong use of something | Full Walkthrough, Hidden Switch |
| advice that doesn't hold up | Unpopular Opinion, Field Report |
| two options people compare | Head to Head |
| something everyone's talking about | Behind the Buzz |
| a habit overtaken by events | Retirement Notice |
| a measured result from a real client | One Client, Two Numbers |
| a question that keeps arriving | The Real Question |

Whatever the shape: the opening puts the reader inside something, the specifics are real, and `references/channel-rules.md` applies line by line.
