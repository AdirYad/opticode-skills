# Real media

When a post is about an actual product, showing the product working beats any generated picture. For each approved post of that kind, search for real material as well and offer it next to the image option. The buyer chooses.

## Where to look, best first
1. A demo video the maker published.
2. Screenshots from the maker's announcement or from someone's hands-on review.
3. The product's own page, so the buyer can record the screen. Nothing looks more genuine than that.

Files go to `posts/YYYY-MM-DD/media/` under names that say what they show. Press material from the maker and the buyer's own recordings are fine to use. A stranger's photo is not.

## Things that go wrong
- Plenty of product pages only offer logos. A logo isn't a post image, so tell the buyer to record the screen instead.
- Open every file you downloaded. Very small files tend to be icons. Delete what isn't useful.
- Some posts simply have nothing real to show, and that's fine.

## Skip this step
For opinion posts with no specific product, or products with nothing public yet.
