# How to learn the buyer's voice

Run once, from the samples the buyer supplied in `voice/samples/`. Write the result to `voice/voice-dna.md`. It is the imitation target: writers and QA read it so posts sound like the buyer, not like AI.

**Every pattern must have a word-for-word quote from a sample as proof. Do not invent traits. If it's not in the samples, it is not in the file.** With fewer than 10 samples, mark the file `confidence: low` at the top.

## Sections of voice-dna.md

### 1. Opening moves
Name 3-6 ways the buyer really starts a post (a threat to money or time, a knowledge gap, an imagined scene, shared recognition, a shock fact, a personal entry), each with quotes. Note where the reader's pain sits and how late a brand name appears.

### 2. Sentence rhythm
Line length, how often a line breaks, punch then expand, fragments as beats, blank lines between idea groups.

### 3. Signature phrases
Recurring transitions and closers, quoted. How the buyer states a limit or a catch (inline or with a label). Plural or singular address. Words they avoid.

### 4. Emoji habits
How many per post, where, what each one tags. Zero is a valid answer.

### 5. Explaining hard things
How the buyer makes a complex idea simple: everyday objects, analogies, comparisons. Quotes.

### 6. Closers and CTAs
How posts end. Signature. What they ask the reader to do.

### 7. AI smell blocklist
Things that would never come from this buyer. Always include: tidy threes, "בעולם של היום", hedging ("ייתכן ש"), significance inflation, an em dash, a period on every line, a brand name as the first word. Add what the samples show the buyer avoids.

### 8. Quick test
Five yes or no questions a reviewer can apply to a draft, built from the sections above.
