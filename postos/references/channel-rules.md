# Channel rules (every post)

Formatting by `brand.json` `platform`:

| platform | bold | notes |
|---|---|---|
| whatsapp | `*bold*` | italic `_like this_` for an exact prompt or quote |
| telegram | `**bold**` | no HTML |
| other | no markup | plain text |

1. **Every line starts with a Hebrew word.** A line that starts with English, an emoji or a symbol breaks RTL in the app. Check every line, bullets too. A bold or italic marker at the start of a line doesn't count, since the app hides it. `Canva התחברה לג'מיני` becomes `השבוע Canva התחברה לג'מיני`.
2. No em or en dashes and no hyphens at all. Prefix + English or a digit is rewritten: `בכלי AI`, not with a hyphen. A dash is a disqualification.
3. Short lines, one idea per line. A blank line between idea groups.
4. Real punctuation: a period closes a thought, commas, question marks. Not a period on every line, and never a post with none.
5. No quote marks; no source URLs in the body.
6. Emoji count per `voice/voice-dna.md`; at line end, spread out, never in a cluster at the end.
7. Hebrew transliteration where it is common: טוקנים, סוכנים, פרומפט. Product names stay in English.
8. Length: `brand.json` `wordRange`, default 60 to 160 words. Depth is not length; every word works.
9. Address per `brand.json` `address` and the voice samples.
10. If `brand.json` has a `signature`, it closes the post. If it has `joinLine` and `joinLink`, they come after the signature, word for word, never reworded.
11. Always `https://`.
12. Offer facts (price, deadline, inclusions) are read from the live page the post links to, right now. Never from notes or memory.
